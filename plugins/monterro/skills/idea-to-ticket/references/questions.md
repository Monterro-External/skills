# Interview questions

Ask only what the PM has not already said. One question per message, in this order, wait for each answer.

## S (at most four)

1. **Where and when**: which surface, which role or tenant, which conditions. For a missing capability, the steps that end where the user gets stuck.
2. **Current and expected**: one line each, in the user's terms.
3. **Regression clause**: the one neighbouring behaviour that must keep working, as "when X, the system shall continue to Y". Propose one from context; the PM confirms or corrects.
4. **Second source**: who else sees this.

## M and L (at most five, in this priority)

1. **Who**: segment or persona with the underlying problem, and how they cope today.
2. **Evidence**: what exists now (support, sales calls, research, usage) or nothing yet.
3. **Outcome**: what customers would do differently, and what that ladders to.
4. **Non-goals**: what a reader would assume is included and is not.
5. **Door**: anything on the L list in step 3.

For L, also note that a named decision owner and the migration and rollback intent are needed before the gate. Placeholders in the ticket, not questions now.
