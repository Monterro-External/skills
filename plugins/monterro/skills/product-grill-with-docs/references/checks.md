# Readiness checks

Section 7 of `product-ticket-standard.md` at **v1.8**, restated as pass/fail rules, and the fallback when the standard itself is not reachable in the session. Numbers are stable; skills and evals refer to them. Applies-to column: S means the check runs for S-tier tickets; all others run for M and L only.

Where this file and the standard disagree, the standard wins and this file is wrong: say so in the verdict's Notes rather than grading against both.

| # | Check | Passes when | Applies |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Intent | One sentence, describing one change. Two verbs joined by "and" is usually two changes | S M L |
| 2 | Evidence | Every claim in Problem and evidence has a link or an attachment. The original signal is present verbatim, with source, author and date. "Link needed" or a named-but-unlinked source fails | S M L |
| 3 | Outcome and success criteria | The outcome is stated as changed customer behaviour rather than as something shipped. At M that is one line of prose and there is nothing further to grade. At L each success criterion is also measurable with the bar as a number, says nothing about technology, and describes something a user could observe; a sample size is not a threshold, and no criterion may promise what a product decision elsewhere in the ticket makes impossible | M L |
| 4 | Non-goals | Populated, and at least one names something a reasonable reader would otherwise assume is in scope | M L |
| 5 | No implementation | Nothing from the prohibition list (modules, services, schemas, endpoints, frameworks, file names, implementation steps, estimates, resourcing) as a requirement; kept ideas are marked non-binding | S M L |
| 6 | Scenarios | Every acceptance scenario set includes an error case and a regression (upset-if-broken) case | M L |
| 7 | No vague adjectives | In the author's own prose anywhere in the ticket, Problem and evidence included: none of fast, scalable, secure, intuitive, robust, seamless, or their kin, without a number beside it. Verbatim quoted signal is exempt wherever it appears: it describes pain in the customer's words, and "slow" in a quote describes the pain rather than specifying the fix. Prose the author writes around a quote is theirs, not the customer's | S M L |
| 8 | Placeholders and open questions | No unresolved placeholder or template hint text, and three or fewer open questions. The cap is a handover gate: a fourth question means the ticket is still in discovery, not that the writing is wrong. An owner and a needed-by date per question were retired in v1.7 and are not required | M L |
| 9 | Experience reference | Any design reference states its precedence beside the link, illustrative or binding. No reference at all is a valid state and passes: it says the solution space is still open. At L the Experience reference section exists and says which of the three it is. `experience_precedence` was retired as a frontmatter field in v1.7, so read the prose and never fail a ticket for the missing field | M L |
| 10 | Tier | Content matches tier, per the v1.7 templates. S has Intent, Problem and evidence, and Behaviour with current, expected, a regression clause in the form "when X, the system shall continue to Y", and an optional reference line, and nothing more. M has Intent, Problem and evidence, Outcome as prose, Scope, Decisions made as bullets, Acceptance scenarios, Open questions. L has Intent, Problem and evidence, Outcome and success criteria, Scope, the Product decisions table with rejected alternatives, Migration and rollback intent, Acceptance scenarios, Experience reference, Open questions, plus `decision_owner` and `outcome_metric` as fields. Appetite, Ideas parked, a Pre-mortem and red-team section and a Decision owner section were retired in v1.7: their presence is a stale-template finding, not padding, and the fix is to fold the content into evidence and assumptions. A genuine mismatch is fixed by splitting or upgrading, never by padding | S M L |
| 11 | Consistency | No contradiction with CONTEXT.md capabilities, principles or non-goals, and no contradiction with an accepted PDR in the ticket's product area. A capability the ticket deliberately changes is not a contradiction: it passes when the ticket names the CONTEXT.md line it changes and says so in Assumptions and decided defaults, and it fails when the ticket has not noticed. Never demand the CONTEXT.md edit as a condition of handover: that file describes what we ship and is amended when the change ships. Silence does not fail; it is noted | S M L |
| 12 | Vocabulary | Every domain term appears in the engineering glossary with the meaning the ticket uses. A term the glossary lacks does not fail; it is noted. Glossary unreachable: skipped, and skipped counts as failing | S M L |

Two notes on judgment:

- Check 5 is about binding language. "The Tenants table" is a product surface, allowed. "The tenants endpoint" is implementation, rewritten at the observable altitude or kept as a line marked non-binding.
- Check 11 reads CONTEXT.md's "Where this file is silent" section before noting a silence: one CONTEXT.md already admits is not news.
