# monterro-product-process plugin

Product-side skills for the Monterro product development process, plus the engineering-side checks that wrap it. Engineering-side is Matt Pocock's skills plugin, unchanged; this plugin produces the Product Ticket that his `grill-with-docs` and `wayfinder` pick up, and reviews what his `implement` builds against the Spec.

Skills graduate into this plugin from `../skills/src/` one at a time, once its eval run passes. The evals run on a sample company's repos; nothing in the plugin depends on them. Every skill here follows the lean rule: one job, SKILL.md on one screen, no environment detection, reads the company's own files, config from `/monterro-product-process:setup`, and `disable-model-invocation: true` in the frontmatter so it runs only when a person types the slash command.

| Skill | Job |
|---|---|
| `/monterro-product-process:setup` | Once per product repo. Asks which tracker tickets live in and where the reference files are, writes `.claude/monterro.json`, scaffolds what is missing |
| `/monterro-product-process:idea-to-ticket` | A PM's own idea, observation or defect into a Product Ticket in the right tier's template |
| `/monterro-product-process:interview-summary` | One raw interview transcript into the research note, written blind to the rest of the round, every quote timestamped |
| `/monterro-product-process:interview-synthesis` | A round's notes into the synthesis report: themes with two or more independent voices, single voices kept apart, never a ticket |
| `/monterro-product-process:support-synthesis` | A period of the support queue into the synthesis report: themes by what the customer was trying to do, counted from a per-ticket table rather than tags, the accounts absent from each theme named |
| `/monterro-product-process:support-dig` | One hypothesis against the whole queue: every matching ticket by account and month, the control account, quotes with ids, where else to look. Writes nothing |
| `/monterro-product-process:ask-product` | One question from anyone in the company about how the product works, answered from the product's own sources: a verdict from a closed set, a confidence derived rather than felt, and a citation on every claim. Refuses a question about a named end user's or account's live state and refuses a ship date, routing each to where the answer lives. Comes back `unknown-gap` when nothing citable exists rather than composing an answer from priors, and logs every exchange so the gaps cluster. Reads only, files nothing, starts no other skill |
| `/monterro-product-process:feature-request-ledger` | A sweep of the request channel into the one living ledger: messages clustered by the ask, named accounts and claimed repeats counted apart, in-thread decisions kept, status per ask, paste-ready replies. Never a ticket |
| `/monterro-product-process:feedback-synthesis` | A period of NPS comments, reviews and in-product feedback into the synthesis report: themes by intent across the customer admin's voice and the end user's, the end users who asked for nothing in their own section, never promoted. Reads whichever channels setup configured |
| `/monterro-product-process:sales-synthesis` | A period of call transcripts and lost-deal debriefs into the synthesis report: themes ranked by where in the call they were raised and what they cost, market kept apart from book, losses we chose named |
| `/monterro-product-process:account-synthesis` | A period of CSM notes and escalations into the account report: one section per account, workarounds without a ticket, the renewal sheet, open commitments, shared problems ranked by ARR share and never by mentions |
| `/monterro-product-process:synthesis` | The cross-source pass over every report, the ledger and the cohorts: findings two or more independent channel families show, the ones one channel shows kept apart, the patterns nobody asked for, contradictions between reports. One more report into the same folder |
| `/monterro-product-process:signal-triage` | The Triage state and the reports' For triage lines into one embryo per problem in `backlog`, typed and tiered, signal preserved; merges into the ticket that exists, declines against a non-goal or a release note, never touches a ticket past triage. Prints the plan and waits before writing |
| `/monterro-product-process:brainstorm-ideas` | Divergence then convergence over one customer pain, ending in idea cards with their riskiest assumptions named and a discard list kept |
| `/monterro-product-process:advance-ticket` | Read a ticket, diagnose where it stands against the standard, and name and prepare the cheapest next action toward handover. Works inside discovery, never moves the ticket out of it |
| `/monterro-product-process:solution-tournament` | Rank alternative solutions for a ticket by blind pairwise judging against the ticket's own outcome, evolving the strongest candidates, ending in a favoured candidate and a validation plan. Refuses when the signals are too thin to judge against |
| `/monterro-product-process:grill-preview` | A dry run of the engineering grill on one ticket. Projects the questions grill-with-docs would ask, sorts each into product (should already be settled), engineering (expected) or boundary with the reason, judges whether the ticket is too big and drafts a split with a structural confidence, and writes one self-contained HTML report to `grill-preview/<ticket-id>.html`. Asks nothing and changes nothing on the ticket. Use before the readiness gate to see whether the grill will take minutes or hours |
| `/monterro-product-process:product-grill-with-docs` | The readiness gate. Checks a ticket against the bundled readiness checks (`references/checks.md`, product ticket standard v1.13, or the company's own standard when setup names one), CONTEXT.md, the decision records and engineering's glossary, then moves it to ready-for-handover or leaves it in discovery with `needs-more-info` and the failing checks named. The only skill that opens that gate |
| `/monterro-product-process:handover-triage` | The second gate, engineering's. Reads a ticket at ready-for-handover against the engineering repo's code, ADRs and glossary, then hands it over on the grilling or the wayfinder route and moves it to in-development, or returns it to discovery with `needs-more-info` and one of four reason codes. Prints the verdict and waits for the engineer. Creates nothing in the engineering team: the Spec or the map is the root and links back. The only skill that moves a ticket out of ready-for-handover |
| `/monterro-product-process:spec-review` | Engineering side, run in the platform repo after `/implement`. Reviews a built change against its Spec issue: the Spec against the handover artifact standard and its Product Ticket, the sub-issues against the requirement IDs, and the diff against both. Runs each requirement's test against the base commit, so a suite that passes for the wrong reason fails the review. Posts the verdict on the Spec issue, fixes nothing, moves no state |

Local development: `claude --plugin-dir ./plugin` from this folder, or from a product repo `claude --plugin-dir <path to this repo>/plugins/monterro-product-process`.
